Belly Brawl

My role GAME DESIGN · LEVEL DESIGN · TECHNICAL DESIGN

The project

Belly Brawl is a four-player couch multiplayer brawler being built in UE5. Inspired by sumo wrestling, players use attacks, abilities, throwables and the arena itself to knock opponents out and be the last one standing.

My contribution

I design combat, player interactions and arenas, and build interactive level elements and gameplay features in UE5 Blueprints. I also prototype ideas early so I can test and iterate quickly in playtests.
